## How to Deposit Money in VK999
The payment screen may look slightly different after an app update, but the basic process usually remains similar.
### Step 1: Log In to Your Account
Open the application and enter the registered mobile number, username or account ID. Type the correct password and complete any required verification.
Users facing an account access problem should follow the instructions on the dedicated [VK999 Login](/vk999-login/) page.
### Step 2: Open the Wallet Section
After logging in, look for an option labelled:
Wallet
Deposit
Recharge
Add Funds
Cashier
Balance
Tap the relevant option to open the payment section.
### Step 3: Select a Payment Method
Choose one of the payment methods currently displayed inside the account. Do not assume that a previously available method is still active.
The platform may provide different instructions for each method, so read the payment screen carefully before continuing.
### Step 4: Enter the Deposit Amount
Type the amount you want to add. Make sure it falls within the minimum and maximum limits shown on the screen.
Avoid entering commas, symbols or extra spaces unless the form automatically formats the amount.
### Step 5: Review the Receiving Details
The platform may display a mobile wallet number, bank details, merchant account or payment instructions.
Check:
Account title
Mobile wallet number
Bank name
Account number
Deposit amount
Reference instructions
Payment expiry time
Do not continue when the details look incomplete or different from the verified account screen.
### Step 6: Send the Payment
Open the selected wallet or banking application and enter the receiving information exactly as displayed.
Review the number and amount before confirming the transfer. A payment sent to the wrong account may be difficult to recover.
### Step 7: Save the Transaction Reference
After sending the payment, the wallet or bank should provide a transaction ID, reference number or receipt.
Save this information because it may be required to confirm the deposit.
Users can take a screenshot of the completed transaction, but they should hide unrelated personal information before sharing it with verified support.
### Step 8: Submit the Deposit Request
Return to the platform and enter the requested transaction details. These may include:
Transaction ID
Sender mobile number
Deposited amount
Payment method
Date and time
Receipt image
Tap Confirm or Submit after reviewing the information.
### Step 9: Check the Wallet Balance
The amount may appear instantly or after a short verification period. Refresh the wallet or reopen the application if the balance does not update immediately.
Do not submit duplicate requests for the same transaction unless verified support instructs you to do so.
